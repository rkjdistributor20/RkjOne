## Fiuu findings

- The BR001 Production payment dialog currently labels QR as `QR cawangan`, confirming that the live outlet flow is still static/manual QR rather than Fiuu VT dynamic QR.
- The payment dialog was opened and cancelled safely. No payment, receipt, transaction or stock movement was created.
- Static DuitNow QR assets for the 36 registered Fiuu stores were previously decoded and verified. Static QR remains a manual-confirmation fallback; it cannot automatically prove payment.
- The branch contains the BR001-only Fiuu VT App-to-App flow for official package `com.rms.mobile.razervt`: SALE → STATUS → explicit cashier confirmation → one atomic sale/receipt/stock update.
- Fiuu VT v1.2 does not provide a signed deep-link response. `STATUS=00` is therefore stored as `REPORTED`, not automatically finalized as `PAID`.
- Fully automatic confirmation remains blocked until Fiuu supplies an authenticated server status API or signed callback.
- The official Fiuu VT app was previously shown as incompatible on the BR001 tablet. Do not substitute an unrelated `Fiuu VT+` application.
- No OPA Application Code, Secret Key, secure retrieval instruction or authenticated status contract was found in the registered merchant email. Store/static-QR activation does not equal delivery of dynamic OPA credentials.
- The Fiuu VT Production migration and VT columns/RPC are not present in Production. This is intentional while open shifts, unresolved payment records, device readiness and settlement UAT remain unresolved.

## Physical UAT still required

Complete these steps on BR001 before any real outlet use:

1. Connect the tablet by USB with **Developer options → USB debugging** enabled, approve the RSA prompt, and verify it appears under `adb devices -l`; or pair **Wireless debugging** on the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Install/activate the exact official Fiuu VT package through Fiuu or Managed Google Play. Stop if Google Play still marks the device incompatible.
3. Log in using the BR001 staff account. Do not create another shift; join the existing test shift and have an AM/OM/Admin approve the membership.
4. Verify product loading, opening-stock SOP, reject-stock states, shift summary and session-expiry recovery without taking payment.
5. With Finance present and Fiuu VT confirmed compatible, perform one low-value controlled UAT payment. Match amount, Transaction ID, order ID, MYR currency, channel, RKJ receipt, stock movement, shift total, Fiuu settlement and bank credit.
6. Test cancel, timeout, pending, app-kill/reopen, network loss, duplicate callback/deep-link and late success before widening the allowlist.

## Rollout gate

Keep `POS_QR_PAYMENT_MODE=manual` in Production and do not apply the Fiuu VT migration or expand `POS_FIUU_VT_BRANCHES` beyond BR001 until all physical UAT and Finance reconciliation steps pass. BR002–BR036 must remain on verified static QR/manual confirmation during the pilot.
