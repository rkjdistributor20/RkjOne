BR001 has passed the **authenticated staff, checkout-path and controlled static-QR payment UAT**, but it is **not ready for signed OPA dynamic payment or wider outlet rollout**. Farah's BR001 shift membership is `ACTIVE`, the fresh tablet login succeeds, the sale gate opens, and a controlled RM2.50 DuitNow payment was recorded successfully through the current static/manual confirmation flow. The payment created transaction `TX-20261021-00001`, updated shift `SH-20261021-0001`, deducted the expected stock and was reconciled manually by Finance. This proves the RKJ One sale pipeline, not automatic provider confirmation. No Production migration, Fiuu environment change or Fiuu deployment was performed.

The later Preview VT payment adds a second, narrower result: atomic recovery of
an externally confirmed paid intent passes exactly once, but native automatic
confirmation remains unproven because no matching SALE/STATUS callback was
received. Wider rollout remains blocked by that callback gap, the unmatched
earlier provider transaction and settlement/bank reconciliation.
